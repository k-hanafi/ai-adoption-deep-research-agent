## Prompt-safe cues (baked into drafts)

- **jobs:** ATS, company careers/jobs, job boards, LinkedIn job posts
- **owned:** company blog/docs/about/newsroom on company domain; company-hosted podcasts
- **third_party:** news, wires, platform podcasts, YouTube, vendor case studies, LinkedIn posts

## Priority exclusivity

Careers/ATS → jobs (even on company DNS). Wires/vendor/YouTube → third_party. Company CMS → owned.
