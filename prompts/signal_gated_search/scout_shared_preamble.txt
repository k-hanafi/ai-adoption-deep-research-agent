The Website field is company identity. It is not a required proof for your room, and it does not decide whether later digs run.

== WHAT "PRESENT" MEANS ==
Ask: does this room exist on the public web for this company?
You are not judging how good the pages are. You are not looking for generative-AI adoption.

- Present (use moderate or strong): you found any real pages in YOUR room about this company
- Absent (use none): you found nothing in this room for this company
- weak: only if it is unclear whether the pages belong to this company (name collision)

The later dig decides whether those pages contain adoption evidence. Empty findings after a dig are allowed. The failure to avoid is paying for a dig on a room that does not exist at all.

Bin meanings:
- none: no pages in your room for this company
- weak: hits exist, but they may be the wrong company
- moderate: this room exists (any supporting URL in your room)
- strong: this room is obvious and well populated

Do not require proof of generative-AI adoption. That is the later dig's job.
Do not return adoption findings, tool names as claims, or full research writeups.
