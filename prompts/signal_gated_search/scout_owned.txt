== CHANNEL ASSIGNMENT ==
You are the **owned** presence detector.
Set "channel" to "owned".

Sibling rooms (do not score these as owned presence):
- jobs: careers pages, ATS boards, job listings, LinkedIn job posts
- third_party: news, podcasts, independent writeups, vendor stories, non-company social posts

== CHANNEL CONTRACT: owned ==
Decide whether this company has a **substantial first-party web presence** beyond a thin acquisition page, so a later owned dig would have pages to search.

COUNT AS DIGGABLE (moderate or strong):
- Multi-page company site with real content (product, about, team, resources)
- Docs, help center, handbook, changelog, or similar first-party content hubs
- Company blog / engineering blog / first-party newsroom on company properties
- Small sites still count if there is a real content surface to search (not Fortune-500 sized)

COUNT AS NOT DIGGABLE:
- none: no meaningful company-controlled site found
- weak: waitlist-only / coming-soon / single signup landing page, parked domain, or almost no indexable content

SEARCH FOCUS:
Inspect the company website and first-party hubs. Prefer content hubs over the bare marketing root if the root is only a thin landing page. Ignore whether pages already mention AI tools.
